    <div class="service-card featured">

      <p>

        monitoring programs, implementation plans, project concepts, and defensible reporting.
      </p>
      <ul>
        <li>TMDL implementation planning</li>
        <li>Pollutant source and loading evaluations</li>
        <li>Compliance tracking and annual reporting</li>
        <li>Implementation project prioritization</li>
        <li>Technical support for grant-funded water quality projects</li>
      </ul>
    </div>

    <div class="service-card featured">
      <div class="service-label">Core Service</div>
      <h3>Water Quality Monitoring Design</h3>
      <p>
        Monitoring programs designed to answer management questions, support regulatory
        compliance, and produce data that can guide real-world decisions.
      </p>
      <ul>
        <li>Surface water, reservoir, groundwater, and recycled water monitoring plans</li>
        <li>Sampling design, parameters, locations, and frequency</li>
        <li>QA/QC procedures and field data workflows</li>
        <li>Trend analysis and data interpretation</li>
        <li>Public-facing summaries, graphics, and dashboards</li>
      </ul>
    </div>

      <p>
        Data-driven support for managing municipal production wells, improving reliability,
        and prioritizing limited capital funds.
      </p>
      <ul>
        <li>Well and pump performance testing programs</li>
        <li>Specific capacity and efficiency trend analysis</li>
        <li>Well rehabilitation prioritization</li>
        <li>Capital improvement planning support</li>
        <li>Standardized well maintenance and testing protocols</li>
      </ul>
    </div>

      <p>
        Practical planning and owner’s-advisor support for well rehabilitation, pump
        replacement, test wells, and replacement well projects.
      </p>
      <ul>
        <li>Well rehabilitation scopes of work</li>
        <li>Inspection, cleaning, redevelopment, and pump testing recommendations</li>
        <li>Test well and replacement well planning</li>
        <li>Contractor and consultant procurement support</li>
        <li>Technical review of hydrogeologic recommendations</li>
      </ul>

      </p>
      <ul>
        <li>GSP annual report support</li>
        <li>Groundwater elevation and hydrograph analysis</li>
        <li>Groundwater dependent ecosystem monitoring plans</li>
        <li>Model results interpretation</li>
        <li>Public-facing groundwater data summaries</li>
